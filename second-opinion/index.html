Second opinion

Before you decide, ask a second consultant

An independent review of your records and the plan you have been given, so you can decide with confidence. You do not have to transfer your care.

Book a second opinion

Who it helps

  • You have been advised to have surgery, such as a hysterectomy or fibroid surgery, and want to understand your options
  • You are not sure the diagnosis or treatment plan is right for you
  • You have had results that you do not fully understand
  • You want reassurance from a consultant who will take time to listen

What you receive

  • A review of your reports, scans and plan before the call
  • A 60-minute video consultation to discuss your questions
  • A written summary of the consultant's opinion
  • Suggested questions to take back to your own doctor

What it is not

A second opinion is advice. It does not replace your own treating team, and it does not mean you must change doctor or hospital.

What to prepare

  • Scan and ultrasound reports, and images if you have them
  • Blood tests, pathology and previous letters
  • The plan or recommendation you were given
  • Your questions and worries
