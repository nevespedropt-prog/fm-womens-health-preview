Package time is used across calls as you need it: for example an initial consultation, then follow-ups as results come in. Records are reviewed before each call. How long a package stays valid, and what happens to unused time, will be set out in the terms before you pay.

How you pay

You see the price before you book. Payment is by bank transfer in naira. After you choose a time you receive the account details and a payment reference, and your booking is confirmed once the payment is received. Someone else, such as a relative, can pay for you.

What is included

  • Consultation time by secure video
  • A review of the records you share, before each call
  • A written summary after each consultation in the packages

What is not included

  • Tests, scans, medicines and treatment, which are provided and charged by local providers
  • Emergency care
