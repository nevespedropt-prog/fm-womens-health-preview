How you pay

You see the price before you book. Payment options will be confirmed before launch, and will include card payment in pounds or dollars, and bank transfer. A relative abroad can pay for you.

What is included

  • Consultation time by secure video
  • A review of the records you share, before each call
  • A written summary after each consultation

What is not included

  • Tests, scans, medicines and treatment, which are provided and charged by local providers
  • Emergency care
