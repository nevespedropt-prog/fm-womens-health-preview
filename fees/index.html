Fees

Clear fees, shown before you pay

All fees are in pounds sterling (GBP) and US dollars (USD). The figures below are provisional ranges until the consultant confirms the exact prices.

ServiceLengthFee (GBP)Fee (USD)
Entry review
A short video call to understand your symptoms and decide the right pathway.		 20 min £90 to £120 $120 to $160 Book
Initial consultation (video)
A full consultation covering your history, results you already have, and a clear plan.		 45 min £175 to £250 $230 to $330 Book
Second opinion
Your records and treatment plan reviewed by a consultant, a video call and a written summary.		 60 min £225 to £350 $300 to $460 Book
Follow-up (video)
Review results and progress, and coordinate care with your local hospital.		 30 min £110 to £180 $145 to $240 Book
Short follow-up
A quick check-in for a result, a symptom update or a specific question.		 15 min £75 to £110 $100 to $145 Book

How you pay

You see your fee on each time slot before you book. Payment options will be confirmed before launch, and will include card payment in pounds or dollars, and bank transfer. A relative abroad can pay for you.

What is included

  • The consultation itself, by secure video
  • A review of the records you share
  • A written summary for second opinions

What is not included

  • Tests, scans, medicines and treatment, which are provided and charged by local providers
  • Emergency care
