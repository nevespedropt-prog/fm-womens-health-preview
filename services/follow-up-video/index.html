What happens

A private video consultation of 30 to 45 minutes with the consultant. You can share any reports or results securely after you book.

What to bring

  • Reports, scans or blood tests
  • A list of your medicines
  • A short note of your symptoms and your questions

This service does not provide emergency care. In an emergency, go to your nearest emergency department.
