Free consultation

Book this consultation

What happens

A short private video call of 15 to 20 minutes. The consultant listens to the concern you bring, gives general advice and guidance about it, and helps you decide which consultation or package suits you. It is not a full assessment, it does not replace a consultation, and it does not include a diagnosis or a prescription.

What to bring

  • Reports, scans or blood tests
  • A list of your medicines
  • A short note of your symptoms and your questions

This service does not provide emergency care. In an emergency, go to your nearest emergency department.
