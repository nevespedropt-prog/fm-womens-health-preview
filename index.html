Why this matters

You know your body. You deserve to be heard.

Fibroids are more common in Black women and often start earlier and cause more symptoms. Many women have been told for years that heavy, painful periods are normal. In pregnancy and childbirth, Black women in several countries face worse outcomes, and too many say their concerns were not taken seriously.

FM Women's Health exists to change that experience: an unhurried consultation, a plain-English explanation, and a clear plan that you understand and agree with.

How it works

Five simple steps

  1. 1

    Choose and book

    Pick a service and a time in your own time zone. The fee is shown before you pay.

  2. 2

    Share your records

    Send reports and scans through a secure step, not by email or a contact form.

  3. 3

    Meet the consultant

    A private video consultation, wherever you are in Nigeria, the UK or beyond.

  4. 4

    Get your plan

    Clear advice, and a written summary for second opinions.

  5. 5

    Follow-up and local care

    Tests and treatment coordinated with a partner hospital near you.
